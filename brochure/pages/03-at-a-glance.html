Evalezy At a glance

The whole product, on one page

From question paper to checked copies in four steps.

You set up once per paper and upload the pile. Evalezy does the first check of every copy. Nothing reaches a student until a teacher releases it.

  1. 01

    Upload the question paper

    It is read into questions, with their marks and sections. Fix anything in a minute.

    No retyping

  2. 02

    Set the marking scheme

    Accept the AI’s draft for each question, edit it, or write your own with model answers.

    Used for every copy

  3. 03

    Upload every copy at once

    Up to 200 PDFs. Names and roll numbers are read and matched to your students.

    Unsure? It asks you

  4. 04

    Review, then release

    Marks fill in live. Change any mark, then release. Students get their checked copy by email.
