What makes Evalezy different

Not a score table. The copy itself, checked.

Most AI graders return a number per question. Evalezy writes back onto the student’s own pages, the way a teacher would, so students see exactly where marks were won and lost.

  1. 1

    The total, circled

    Marks are added up and circled at the top right of page one, the way a teacher does. Here: 38 out of 80.

  2. 2

    A tick on what earned marks

    Drawn on the line itself with a slightly uneven hand, so it reads as pen, not stamp.

  3. 3

    Marks per question, in the margin

    Every question gets its marks written beside it, in half-mark steps where the scheme allows.

  4. 4

    A cross, with the correct answer

    “Wrong answer. Correct: Indus.”

  5. 5

    A note where marks were lost

    Incomplete answers get a short note on what was missing, so the student knows what to fix.

  6. Same copy, every time. The pen is seeded from the file, so re-rendering a copy draws exactly the same marks.
