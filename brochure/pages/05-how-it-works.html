Evalezy How it works

Three steps for you · eight for Evalezy · then your review

What happens to every copy.

You set the paper up once. Evalezy reads, marks and writes on each copy, with steps that stop it from guessing. Nothing reaches a student until you release it.

Your partBefore

  1. 1

    Upload the paper

    Read into questions with their marks and sections.

  2. 2

    Set the marking scheme

    Accept the AI draft, edit it, or write your own.

  3. 3

    Upload the copies

    Up to 200 PDFs. Names read and matched; unsure ones wait for you.

Evalezy’s part · for every copy

Stop at any time; a stopped check is not charged

  1. Reading the copy

  2. 1

    Read the page layout

    Each page is straightened, cleaned and contrast-boosted, and the position of every written line is found.

  3. 2

    Read the handwriting

    A vision model reads every line as written, three pages at a time.

    Told never to invent text.

    Lines it cannot read are marked unreliable.

  4. 3

    Check the copy is readable

    If most pages are too blurred or blank, it stops with a clear reason.

    Unreadable? It stops.

    No marks guessed · not charged
  5. 4

    Second read for maths

    Unsure maths lines (up to four) go to a dedicated equation reader.

  6. Marking the copy

  7. 5

    Find each answer, by content

    On copies of four pages or more, Evalezy works out which pages hold which answer, so answers written out of order are still found.

    No zero on a first miss.

    If an answer looks unattempted, the whole copy is searched again.

  8. 6

    Mark each question

    Against its criteria, in half-mark steps, with the student’s own words quoted. Marks appear on your screen question by question.

    Low confidence? Asked again, and flagged for you.

  9. 7

    Make the marks add up

    Capped at the maximum; one score, one clear note per question.

  10. 8

    Write on the copy

    Ticks, crosses, corrections, notes and marks drawn on the student’s pages in a handwriting-style red pen, with the total circled on page one.

Your partAfter

  1. 4

    Review

    A bell alert and an email when the batch is done. See the answer as read, marks, feedback and criteria per question. Change any mark: your edit is kept.

  2. 5

    Release

    Students get their checked copy by email. Until then they see “Results pending”.
