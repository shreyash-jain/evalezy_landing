Built to be trusted with marks

Careful where it matters.

We do not publish an accuracy percentage; it depends on handwriting, scans and your scheme. Here is what Evalezy does instead to keep marks right.

Answers found by content

Students answer out of order. Each answer is matched to its question by what it says, not by the number written beside it.

Maths gets a second read

Equations and working are read from the page; lines it is unsure of go to a dedicated equation reader.

Never guesses an unreadable page

If most of a copy cannot be read, the check stops with a clear reason. No invented marks, and no charge.

Marks that add up

Criteria always sum to the question’s marks, marks come in half steps, and every deduction gets one clear note.

Never guesses a student

No name, a weak match or two close names? The copy waits for you to pick. It is never filed under a guessed name.

Teachers have the last word

Marks are a draft until released. A teacher’s edit is never overwritten, even if the copy is checked again.

Honest limits

  1. No accuracy percentage. Judge it on your own copies in a demo. Every mark shows the answer as read and the reason, and low‑confidence questions are flagged for review.
  2. Diagrams, maps and graphs are judged by their labels and written explanation, not as drawings. Keep a teacher on those questions.
  3. English answer sheets. Built and tested on English copies. Hindi-medium or another language? Send us sample copies and we will tell you honestly how it does.
  4. Not instant. 1–8 minutes a copy, several at once. First results in about 10 minutes; 100 copies in about 2–3 hours.

Data & privacy

  • Answer sheets, marks and checked copies live inside your institute’s account.
  • Students see only their own result, and only after release. The server enforces it.
  • Pages go to the AI and equation‑reading providers Evalezy uses; each step gets only what it needs.
  • No SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification and no on‑premise option today.
