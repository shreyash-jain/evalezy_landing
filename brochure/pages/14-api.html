Evalezy API

For developers

Answer sheet evaluation, as an API.

Grade handwritten copies and typed long answers from your own exam system. Get back question-wise marks with reasons, feedback and the red-pen checked copy.

₹1 / $0.01

per handwritten page (1 credit), excl. GST. Typed tests: 1 credit per non-blank long answer; objective answers free.

  1. 1

    Create an exam

    Handwritten or typed, with marks, answer keys and rubrics.

    POST /exams
  2. 2

    Upload and submit

    One PDF per candidate (max 50 MB) and your own candidate id.

    POST /exams/{id}/submissions
  3. 3

    Poll for results

    No webhooks yet: poll the feed of every submission that changed.

    GET /submissions?updated_since=
  4. 4

    Review, then finalize

    Override or approve marks in your app or in the Vacademy dashboard.

    POST /exams/{id}/finalize

Request access

Enabled per institute: ask at evalezy.com/demo. Your admin then creates keys in the Vacademy dashboard.

Key endpoints base https://api.evalezy.com/v1 · X-API-Key header

POST/examsCreate an exam: questions, marks, answer keys, rubrics or model answers
POST/exams/{id}/openOpen a draft exam; questions, marks and answer keys are then fixed
PUT/exams/{id}/questions/{qid}/rubricSet one long answer’s rubric and model answer
POST/candidatesCreate or update up to 2,000 candidates by your own external_id
POST/uploadsPresigned upload URLs for 1 to 100 PDFs, 50 MB each
POST/exams/{id}/submissionsSubmit a copy (upload_id) or typed answers; 202 with a fixed quote
GET/submissions?updated_since=…Every submission that changed, across exams: poll it for results
GET/submissions/{id}/resultMarks, criteria with reasons, feedback, answer as read, confidence
GET/submissions/{id}/checked-copyThe checked copy PDF: ticks, crosses, notes and marks
PATCH/submissions/{id}/questions/{qid}Override one question’s marks (0.5 steps) and feedback; free
POST/exams/{id}/finalizePublish draft marks as final; nothing is final until you call it
POST/credits/quoteQuote the fixed price before you submit, and check your balance covers it

No sandbox: every key is live and every graded copy is billed, so test with a small exam. Not yet: webhooks, phone photos, bulk scans matched by name, Hindi. API exams also appear in the Vacademy dashboard, tagged “Source: API”. Full reference, guides and roadmap: docs.evalezy.com
