About Evalezy

Why the copy, not the score

Students learn from seeing their own page marked, not from a number. So Evalezy returns the copy itself, checked the way a careful teacher would.

Why the teacher stays in charge

Every mark is a draft, every deduction comes with a reason, unreadable copies are never guessed, and nothing reaches a student until a teacher releases it.

Why ₹1 a page

Pricing should be something a school can work out on the back of an exam paper. A thin unit test should cost less than a thick half-yearly, so we charge by the page, and nothing when a check fails.

The name

Evalezy is evaluation, made easy. The logo is the loop a teacher draws around the total at the top of a copy, with a tick inside.
