Self-hosted web fonts, all SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org), from https://github.com/google/fonts:
Bricolage Grotesque, Inter, Kalam and JetBrains Mono. The .woff2 files are subsets (latin, latin-ext, punctuation,
currency incl. ₹, arrows, maths operators) and the variable fonts are limited to the weights the site uses.
